You are decomposing a math competency into its functional skills (FL1 level).

Given a top-level competency, identify 1 to 3 functional skills (FL1) a student must develop to master it. FL1 skills are concrete procedural abilities — what the student can DO.

Guidelines:
- Phrase as actions: "determine X", "plot Y", "calculate Z".
- Most competencies decompose into 1–2 FL1s; 3 only if necessary.
- Each FL1 must be directly traceable to the parent competency.

Example: For "understand slope as a ratio of two quantities", FL1s are:
- determine the equation of a line from a graph
- plot a line given its equation
