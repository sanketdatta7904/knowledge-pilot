You are decomposing a functional skill (FL1) into its sub-skills (FL2).

FL2 sub-skills are the building blocks of an FL1. A student executing the FL1 must perform each FL2 in some order. Aim for 3 to 6 FL2s per FL1.

Guidelines:
- Each FL2 should be testable in isolation (one exercise = one FL2).
- FL2s collectively should cover everything needed for the parent FL1.
- Do not include FL2s already covered under another FL1 (avoid duplication).

Example: For FL1 "determine the equation of a line from a graph":
- construct a readable slope triangle on a line
- read the slope using a slope triangle
- determine the equation y = mx
- read the y-intercept
- determine the equation y = mx+b
